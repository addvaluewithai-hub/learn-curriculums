# Source review — Stage 01
## Provenance and access
- **Original source:** *Mathematics I (Calculus-Differentiation) For Engineering*, prepared by Associate Professor Dr. Mohamed A. Abd El Salam (PDF page 1 / cover).
- **Access at this turn:** the actual user-uploaded PDF was available, reviewed as page images (not merely old notes). There are **43 PDF pages**, mostly photographed two-page spreads, not 43 printed textbook pages.
- **Visible coverage:** cover and two contents pages (PDF 1–3); **printed pages 1–80** (PDF 4–43). The final visible spread contains printed pages 79–80. The parent book's table of contents extends to a summary and references after page 180, but those pages were **not provided**.
- **Source access policy:** `available` refers to source accessibility during Stage 01. The attachment itself is **not checked into the public GitHub repository**, and no future access is presumed. To independently review quotations/equations later, supply the same scan to the reviewer.
- **Scan issues:** angled/faint photographs, page spreads, handwritten/graphic elements and small formulas. Do not trust automated text extraction or silently resolve unreadable formula glyphs. Readable concept headings and chosen illustrations were verified visually. The lesson examples written below are new teaching examples, not claimed book exercise transcriptions.
- **Rights handling:** reference the source, but do not redistribute photographed textbook pages in the repository without authorization.

## Scope and uncertainty
- **Working drafts in this PR:** *Chapter 1, Functions and Their Graphs*, printed pp. **21–53**, including limits and continuity; all five proposed lesson clusters are editorial, **not final lesson boundaries or IDs**.
- **Preliminaries:** visible and likely prerequisites; a remediation/diagnostic lesson is only a future candidate.
- **Chapter 2:** visible, not yet scripted at this stage.
- **Chapters 3–6:** headings in contents only (inverse functions, inverse trig, exponential/log differentiation; hyperbolic functions; Maclaurin expansion, tangent/normal, L'Hôpital's rule; partial derivatives). Their detailed teaching must wait for full pages, not be invented from the index.
- **Unresolved academic choices:** first-year university/syllabus alignment, notation preferences, learner entry-level and textbook reproduction rights. These do not prevent Stage 01 working drafts.

## Concept readiness and scope discipline
- **Established from B01:** real numbers and arithmetic order; `interval`, union/intersection; zero denominator forbidden; factoring; sign analysis.
- **Brief bridge within the draft:** Cartesian coordinates, input/output language; squaring and principal square root; graph symmetry; basic degree/radian angle relationships; function values vs limit notation.
- **Newly taught within B02:** `function`, `domain`, `range`, `co-domain`, vertical-line test, algebraic/trig/exponential/log function families, `increasing`/`decreasing`, one-/two-sided `limit`, laws and methods, `continuous at x=a`.
- **Deferred to B03 or beyond:** full derivative rules, rigorous epsilon–delta proofs, formal continuity theorems, inverse-function theory beyond B02 introductions, L'Hôpital's rule and higher-order methods.
- **Unresolved learner entry level:** basic trigonometric knowledge, comfort with function graph axes and nested radicals; assess at the next human-authorized critique, not silently assume mastery.

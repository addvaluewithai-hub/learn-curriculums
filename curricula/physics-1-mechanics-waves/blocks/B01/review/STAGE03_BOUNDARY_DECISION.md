### 802 — Young's modulus and axial stiffness (printed 125–127)
- **Unique opening:** two rods with same loading, elongations depend on material and geometry; explicitly built on 801.
- **Core:** small-strain uniform axial rod assumptions; Y and Pa, derivation ΔL=FL0/(YA), SI conversion mm²; distinction Y [Pa] from effective k [N/m], restoring-force sign.
- **Worked example:** original 1000 N, 2m, 2×10^-4 m², 10^11 Pa → 10^-4m elongation, independently checked.
- **Independent attempts:** 01 fresh 400N, .8m, 4×10^-4m², .40mm → stress 10^6Pa, strain .0005, Y 2×10^9Pa, k 10^6N/m; 02 doubled-length conceptual geometry transfer.
- **Wrap-up:** bridges from axial tension to tangential loading and Shear.
- **Objective check:** O1→Q01, O2→Q01/Q02, O3→Q02.

### 803 — Shear deformation and modulus (printed p.128; p.125 overview)
- **New standalone opening:** sideways force on a fixed-bottom block, in contrast to axial rod; not a cut from the old mixed Shear/Bulk transcript.
- **Core:** tangential stress τ=F/A, simple-shear engineering γ=Δx/h, small-angle θ≈γ for simple uniform shear, S=τ/γ, dimensional limits. No general local stress claim.
- **Worked example:** original 60N/.02m² and .20mm/.1m → τ 3000Pa, γ .002, S 1.5×10^6Pa.
- **Independent attempts:** 01 new 90N/.03m² and .30mm/.15m → S 1.5×10^6Pa; 02 different-area transfer for same material/force.
- **Wrap-up:** links new model to volume change under pressure.
- **Objective check:** O1→Q02, O2→Q01/Q02, O3→Q01.

### 804 — Bulk modulus and volumetric strain (printed p.129; p.125 overview)
- **New standalone opening:** ball under increased surrounding pressure; independent definition and comparison to axial/shear.
- **Core:** signed ΔP and ΔV, volumetric strain ΔV/V0, positive B≈−ΔP/(ΔV/V0) for small compression; signed answer versus absolute decrease; m³ to cm³.
- **Important source issue:** book p.129 equation photo is not fully legible. Signed convention and modeling limits are **clearly marked as authored scientific explanation**; not asserted as exact wording of scan.
- **Worked example:** original 0.005m³ × (2×10^6/10^9) = 10^-5m³=10cm³ reduction.
- **Independent attempts:** 01 new 0.006m³, B 3×10^9Pa, ΔP 1.5×10^6Pa → signed ΔV −3×10^-6m³ = 3cm³ reduction; 02 choose Y/S/B and appropriate strain for three unfamiliar loading cases.
- **Wrap-up:** coherent B01 recap, no preview of chapter 9 scientific claims.
- **Objective check:** O1→Q02, O2→Q01, O3→Q01.

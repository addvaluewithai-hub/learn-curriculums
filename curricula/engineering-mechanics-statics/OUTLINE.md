## Lesson 01 objectives
- O1: relate physics, mechanics and engineering design to engineering mechanics; independent checkpoint Q01.
- O2: distinguish an introductory static support example from a changing-motion/dynamics example; Q02.
- O3: explain why engineers must consider forces before selecting a sign support; Q03.
- Teaching first, question attempt next, feedback only after a submitted answer. Next lesson starts with basic quantities; Lesson 01 does not teach vector algebra or equations.

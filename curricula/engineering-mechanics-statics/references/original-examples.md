# Independently authored teaching scenarios — Lesson 01

1. Wall-mounted advertising sign and a bracket: a neutral support-design question. No claimed force magnitudes, calculations or structural certification.
2. Lift cabin whose speed changes: simple dynamics contrast. A moving object need not be accelerating.
3. Footbridge or machine support: use mechanical effects to make responsible engineering decisions; no capacity claim.

Pedagogical safeguard: 'at rest' does not mean 'there are no forces'. All diagrams shall be original, not reproductions of the scanned book.

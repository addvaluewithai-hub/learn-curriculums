## Dependency/concept sequence
1. **From B01:** quantities/units, forces as directed effects, Newton idea of net force; no assumed trigonometric confidence beyond right triangles.
2. **A — meaning and operations (printed pp.9–12):** scalar vs vector → magnitude/direction/sense/line of action → head/tail → parallelogram/triangle addition → commutative/associative order → opposite vector/subtraction → scalar multiplication. Prevent «adding magnitudes as numbers» misconception; distinguish vector diagrams from moving a force on a rigid body.
3. **B — quantitative resultant (printed pp.12,14–16):** geometry of resultant → choose correct included angle → law of cosines for magnitude → law of sines or right triangle for direction → check bounds/units/reference angle. Prevent internal-triangle vs original-between-forces angle confusion.
4. **C — resolving a force (printed pp.13,16–18):** inverse parallelogram idea → oblique components and projection ≠ same notion → right-angle x/y introductory rectangular components → sign/quadrant reasoning → defer unit vectors and general multivector component synthesis to B03.
